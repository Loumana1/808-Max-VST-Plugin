# 808 Max

<p align="center">

</p>
808 Max is a free, open-source VST instrument for hip-hop, trap, drill, hyperpop, and electronic producers. It gives you a curated set of usable 808 bass sounds and the few controls that actually matter — saturation, envelope, filter, and a live waveform — then gets out of the way.

No 300-preset browsers. No filler samples. Load it, pick a bass, shape it, finish the beat.

---

## Why it exists

Most 808 plugins make you hunt. You scroll through endless folders, stack three other plugins just to make the sound usable, and lose the idea you came to record.

808 Max is built by a producer who was tired of that workflow. The goal is simple: **trusted 808s, fast results, no bloat.**

---

## What's inside

- **Curated 808s** — hand-picked basses inspired by sounds producers actually reach for (Life, Guitar Hero, Opium, Ghosted, Vendetta, and more)
- **Saturation engine** — Tape, Overdrive, Tube, Transistor, and Distortion
- **ADHRS envelope** — Attack, Decay, Hold, Release, Sustain for punch, glide, and decay
- **Live waveform** — see what you're shaping while you play
- **Reverse playback** — one click for intros, transitions, and texture
- **Clean dual-panel UI** — sound selection on one side, sound design on the other

A visual preset browser is also in development: samples placed in space by decay, distortion, and brightness, so you find a bass by how it *sounds*, not by its filename.

---

## Who it's for

Bedroom producers, beatmakers, and anyone who wants 808s to just work — in the studio or on a session — without a 40-knob instrument getting in the way.
