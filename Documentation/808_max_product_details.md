## 🎚️ Feature List

### Core Features
- **25 Pre-curated 808s** - Hand-picked legendary bass sounds including "Life", "Guitar Hero", "Opium", "Vendetta", "Baby", "Ghosted", "Sanctuary", "Geek", and more

### Installation Instructions
1. **Download** the plugin from [your website/GitHub]
2. **Extract** the ZIP file
3. **Copy** to your DAW's plugin folder:
   - Windows: `C:\Program Files\VST3\` or `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\`
   - macOS: `/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/` or `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/`

## 🔗 Integration & Workflow

### Recommended Workflow
1. **Load 808 Max** in your DAW
2. **Switch to Samples panel** and select from 25 curated 808s
3. **Choose saturation type** from 5 different algorithms
4. **Adjust saturation amount** for desired character
5. **Shape with ADHRS envelope** for dynamic control
6. **Use reverse playback** for creative effects
7. **Tune visually** using real-time waveform display
8. **Record and repeat**

### Tips for Best Results
- **Layer with kicks** for punch
- **Use sidechain** to avoid conflicts
- **Tune to your track's key**
- **Experiment with different saturation types** - Tape for warmth, Distortion for aggression
- **Use reverse playback** for unique intros and effects
- **Save presets** for quick recall
- **Switch between panels** for organized workflow
